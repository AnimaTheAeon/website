Gathering Magic

Every card. Instantly.

A fast, beautiful card search for Magic: The Gathering. The entire card database lives on your device, so every search is immediate and works without a connection.

A full page for every card.

Large image, rules, flavor, artist, legalities and every printing. Swipe between results, or tap for a large-print reading mode.
